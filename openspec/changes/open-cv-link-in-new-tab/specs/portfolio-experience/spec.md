## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Download CV opens in a new tab
The Download CV call to action MUST open its configured destination in a new browser tab while keeping the portfolio page open in its original tab.

#### Scenario: Visitor activates Download CV
- **WHEN** a visitor activates the Download CV call to action
- **THEN** its configured destination opens in a new browser tab
- **AND** the portfolio remains open in the original tab
