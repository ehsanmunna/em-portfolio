## Why

The portfolio's Professional Experience section is currently based on older or placeholder role data. Updating it from the provided PDF will align the portfolio with the latest career history and make the experience narrative accurate for visitors and recruiters.

## What Changes

- Refresh the Professional Experience section copy to match the supplied PDF source material.
- Update the role, company, timeline, and description entries in the portfolio content configuration.
- Preserve the current page structure, visual rhythm, and supporting presentation patterns while replacing outdated experience content.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `portfolio-experience`: Updated professional experience content and supporting narrative for the portfolio's experience timeline.

### Modified Capabilities
- None

## Impact

- Changes the source content used by the experience section in the Next.js client application.
- Affects the content configuration used to render the Professional Experience section, without changing the general layout system.
- Requires a quick validation pass to confirm the updated copy still fits the existing section hierarchy and responsive layout.
